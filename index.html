Research Summary

My research focuses on understanding the co-evolution super-massive black holes and their host galaxies from early times to the rich complexity observed today. I investigate this primarily from an observational perspective, with particular expertise in combining multi-wavelength tracers, from X-rays to radio, to study this.

LoTSS Deep Fields DR1
Value-added radio catalogues

I lead the creation of new multi-wavelength catalogues and value-added radio catalogues for the LoTSS-Deep DR1. These were vital and enabled a wide range of scientific analysis. You can find more information here: LoTSS-Deep.

Cosmic evolution of jet-mode AGN

I presented the first measurement of the evolution of jet-mode AGN out to z = 2.5, finding a dominant new population of jet-mode AGN hosted by star-forming galaxies not previously observed. You can find more information here: Jet-mode AGN
