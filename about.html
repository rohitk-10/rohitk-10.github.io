About Me

I am an Astrophysics PhD student within the Institute for Astronomy at the University of Edinburgh. My primary research interest is in understanding how energy from the supermassive black holes at the centeres of galaxies affect their host galaxies, and how this so called energetic "feedback" shapes the evolution of galaxies across cosmic time.

Since the discovery of a tight correlation between the mass of the central supermassive black holes and that of the central galaxy spheroid, it has become increasingly evident that the energy released by accreting supermassive black holes (known as active galactic nuclei; or AGN) can have a significant impact on the galaxies in which they reside.
